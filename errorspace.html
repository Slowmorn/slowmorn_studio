ErrorSpace

Coming soon

Ignored, it is nothing — debugged, it means something.

Most apps treat errors as flaws to erase. ErrorSpace asks a different question — what if an error were a place you could visit, and grow from?

ErrorSpace reimagines daily reflection as debugging. Each day, a couple of small errors — ERRs — surface: gentle prompts to notice, act, and grow. Resolve one in a quick DEBUG SESSION, and it crystallizes into an artifact that takes its place in your personal galaxy, MY SPACE.

No streak shaming. No endless feeds. Just a quiet, sci-fi-flavored ritual you can finish in two minutes.

  1. 1

    Receive your ERRs

    Each day brings a small mission, framed as an error to debug.

  2. 2

    Run a DEBUG SESSION

    Jot down what you did, or attach a photo as evidence. That's it.

  3. 3

    Earn artifacts

    Every completed debug becomes a glowing planet in your galaxy.

  4. 4

    Grow MY SPACE

    Pan and zoom across a vast canvas that fills in as you keep showing up.

Meet KERNEL

A companion through it all.

KERNEL is a friendly mascot that tends your errors, offers a nudge when you're stuck, and looks back on your week with you. Collect new KERNELs and set them roaming through your space.

§ Privacy Policy
Last updated: August 9, 2026
ErrorSpace does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All data stays on your device.

1. Overview

ErrorSpace ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how ErrorSpace handles information when you use our iOS application.

2. Data we do not collect

ErrorSpace does not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

3. Data stored locally

All data generated by your use of ErrorSpace — including your debug sessions, notes, earned artifacts, your MY SPACE galaxy, KERNEL conversations, and app settings — is stored exclusively on your device using iOS local storage (UserDefaults / SwiftData). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

4. Photos

If you choose to attach a photo as evidence during a debug session, that image is stored only on your device alongside the related entry. Photos are never uploaded to any server, and ErrorSpace only accesses your photo library when you explicitly select an image.

5. Push notifications

ErrorSpace uses local push notifications to send your daily reminders. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers or data transmission. You can disable notifications at any time in your iPhone Settings.

6. Third-party services

ErrorSpace does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

7. Children's privacy

ErrorSpace is suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal data, there are no special considerations regarding children's privacy.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of ErrorSpace after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@slowmorn.com.
