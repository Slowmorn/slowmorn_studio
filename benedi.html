Benedi

Available now

Bless your day with kindness.

Benedi comes from two simple words — bene, meaning good, and di, meaning day. A good day. And the simplest way to make any day good? Kindness.

Every day, Benedi sends you one gentle nudge — a small, real act of kindness you can offer the world. No streaks to protect, no pressure to perform. Just "I'll do it" or "Not today." Either one is fine.

Kindness is the quiet truth that shapes a blessed day. Your kindness turns today into a blessing — and that blessing has a way of finding its way back to you.

§ Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 10, 2026
Benedi does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All data stays on your device.

1. Overview

Benedi ("we," "our," or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Benedi handles information when you use our iOS application.

2. Data we do not collect

Benedi does not collect any personal information. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any contact information
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • Usage analytics or behavioral data
  • Any data transmitted to external servers

3. Data stored locally

All data generated by your use of Benedi — including your kindness history and app settings — is stored exclusively on your device using iOS local storage (UserDefaults / SwiftData). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

4. Push notifications

Benedi uses local push notifications to send your daily kindness reminder. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and do not involve any external servers or data transmission. You can disable notifications at any time in your iPhone Settings.

5. Third-party services

Benedi does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

6. Children's privacy

Benedi is suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal data, there are no special considerations regarding children's privacy.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of Benedi after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello@slowmorn.com.
